# Class I Minor Works — English Checklist

> **Source**: `MWTGe.pdf` — Technical Guidelines on Minor Works Control System
> **Publisher**: Hong Kong Buildings Department (2010/10 edition)
> **Coverage**: Items 1.1 to 1.40 (40 items from Appendix II)
> **Note**: Items 1.41 – 1.62 are referenced in some checklists but NOT described in `MWTGe.pdf` Appendix II (which ends at 1.40). The 2010 edition of the guidelines does not include descriptions for items 1.41–1.62 — these may be in a later amendment not covered by this PDF.

## Class I Minor Works — Building Works Items (1.1 – 1.40)

### 1.1 — Internal Staircase
**Erection or alteration of any internal staircase** that is not used as a means of escape or a means of access for firefighting and rescue, provided that –
- (a) the works do not result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab; and
- (b) the works do not involve the alteration of any other structural elements, except a simply supported beam that –
  - (i) is not of pre-stressed construction; and
  - (ii) is not used to support any column, flat slab or ribbed beam.

### 1.2 — Slab Opening
**Formation of any opening in a slab**, provided that –
- (a) the works do not result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab;
- (b) the works do not involve the alteration of any other structural elements, except a simply supported beam that –
  - (i) is not of pre-stressed construction; and
  - (ii) is not used to support any column, flat slab or ribbed beam; and
- (c) the area of the opening is more than 1 m² but not more than 4.5 m².

### 1.3 — Service Lift Installation/Alteration
**Building works associated with the installation or alteration of any service lift**, provided that –
- (a) the works do not result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab;
- (b) the works do not involve the alteration of any other structural elements, except a simply supported beam that –
  - (i) is not of pre-stressed construction; and
  - (ii) is not used to support any column, flat slab or ribbed beam;
- (c) the rated load of the lift is not more than 250 kg;
- (d) the internal floor area of the lift car is not more than 1 m²; and
- (e) the internal height of the lift car is not more than 1.2 m.

### 1.4 — Stairlift / Lifting Platform
**Building works associated with the installation or alteration of any stairlift or lifting platform**, provided that –
- (a) the works do not result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab; and
- (b) the works do not involve the alteration of any other structural elements, except a simply supported beam that –
  - (i) is not of pre-stressed construction; and
  - (ii) is not used to support any column, flat slab or ribbed beam.

### 1.5 — Removal of Cantilevered-Slab Support Structure
**Removal of any supporting structure for an air-conditioning unit, water cooling tower, solar water heating system or photovoltaic system** located on a cantilevered slab with a span of more than 1 m.

### 1.6 — Protective Barrier Alteration/Removal
**Alteration or removal of any protective barrier** (other than an external reinforced concrete wall or block wall), provided that the works do not result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab.

### 1.7 — Solid Fence Wall (New/Alter)
**Erection or alteration of any solid fence wall**, provided that –
- (a) the wall is erected on-grade; and
- (b) the height of the wall is more than 1.5 m but not more than 5 m.

### 1.8 — External Mesh Fence (New/Alter)
**Erection or alteration of any external mesh fence**, provided that –
- (a) the fence is erected on-grade; and
- (b) the height of the fence is more than 3 m but not more than 10 m.

### 1.9 — Solid Fence Wall (Removal)
**Removal of any solid fence wall**, provided that –
- (a) the wall is erected on-grade; and
- (b) the height of the wall is more than 3 m.

### 1.10 — External Mesh Fence (Removal)
**Removal of any external mesh fence**, provided that –
- (a) the fence is erected on-grade; and
- (b) the height of the fence is more than 5 m.

### 1.11 — Spread Footing
**Construction or alteration of any spread footing** associated with the carrying out of any other minor works item.

### 1.12 — Excavation Works
**Excavation works** associated with the carrying out of any other minor works item.

### 1.13 — Antenna Supporting Structure
**Erection or alteration of any supporting structure for an antenna** on-grade or on a slab (other than a cantilevered slab).

### 1.14 — Radio-communications Supporting Structure
**Erection or alteration of any supporting structure for a radio-communications station** on-grade or on a slab (other than a cantilevered slab).

### 1.15 — External Reinforced Concrete Structure
**Erection, alteration or removal of any external reinforced concrete structure** (other than an external mesh fence or fence wall).

### 1.16 — Metal Gate at Fence Wall/Entrance
**Erection, alteration or repair of any metal gate at a fence wall or at an entrance to a building.**

### 1.17 — Structural Elements Repair
**Repair of any structural elements** (including any column, shear wall, beam, slab, etc.) other than minor works items 1.7 to 1.10.

### 1.18 — Solar Water Heating System Support
**Erection or alteration of any supporting structure for a solar water heating system** on-grade or on a slab (other than a cantilevered slab), provided that –
- (a) the height of the structure is not more than 1.5 m;
- (b) the structure is designed for a solar water heating system at least one thermal collector of which is more than 200 kg in weight; and
- (c) if the thermal collector and the water tank of the system are integrated, the structure is designed for a system the gross weight (when the water tank is in full capacity) of which is more than 100 kg per m² of the ground or slab area.

### 1.19 — Photovoltaic System Support
**Erection or alteration of any supporting structure for a photovoltaic system** on-grade or on a slab (other than a cantilevered slab), provided that –
- (a) the height of the structure is not more than 1.5 m; and
- (b) the structure is designed for a photovoltaic system at least one module of which is more than 200 kg in weight.

### 1.20 — Projecting Signboard
**Erection or alteration of any projecting signboard**, provided that –
- (a) the signboard does not consist of stone;
- (b) the works do not result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab;
- (c) the works do not involve the alteration of any other structural elements;
- (d) the display area of the signboard is more than 10 m² but not more than 20 m²;
- (e) no part of the signboard projects more than 4.2 m from the external wall to which it is fixed; and
- (f) the thickness of the signboard is not more than 600 mm.

### 1.21 — Signboard on Roof
**Erection or alteration of any signboard on the roof of a building**, provided that –
- (a) the signboard does not consist of stone;
- (b) the works do not result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab;
- (c) the works do not involve the alteration of any other structural elements;
- (d) the display area of the signboard is not more than 20 m²;
- (e) no part of the signboard projects beyond the external wall of the building;
- (f) the thickness of the signboard is not more than 600 mm; and
- (g) the distance between any part of the signboard and the level of the roof is not more than 6 m.

### 1.22 — Wall Signboard
**Erection or alteration of any wall signboard**, provided that –
- (a) the works do not result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab;
- (b) the works do not involve the alteration of any other structural elements;
- (c) if the signboard comprises a display system consisting of light emitting diodes, the display area of the signboard is more than 5 m² but not more than 20 m²;
- (d) if the signboard does not comprise any display system consisting of light emitting diodes, the display area of the signboard is more than 10 m² but not more than 40 m²; and
- (e) if the distance between any part of the signboard and the ground is more than 6 m, the signboard does not consist of stone.

### 1.23 — Outdoor Signboard on-grade
**Erection or alteration of any outdoor signboard fixed on-grade** (other than the construction of a spread footing), provided that –
- (a) the display area of the signboard is not more than 20 m²;
- (b) the thickness of the signboard is not more than 600 mm;
- (c) the distance between any part of the signboard and the ground is not more than 6 m; and
- (d) the works do not fall within the description of item 2.21.

### 1.24 — Signboard Removal
**Removal of any signboard** (other than the removal of the spread footing of any outdoor signboard), provided that the works do not fall within the description of item 11 of Part 2 of Schedule 2 or item 2.24, 2.25, 2.26, 2.27, 3.16, 3.17, 3.18, 3.19, 3.20, 3.21 or 3.22.

### 1.25 — Repair of Underground Drain
**Repair of any underground drain**, provided that –
- (a) the works involve an excavation of a depth of more than 1.5 m but not more than 3 m;
- (b) the distance between any point of the excavation and any structure or building is at least equal to the depth of the excavation;
- (c) the works do not involve any excavation within area number 1 or 3 of the scheduled areas;
- (d) the works do not involve the last manhole;
- (e) if the works are carried out beside the crest of a slope with a gradient of not more than 30 degrees, the distance between any point of the excavation and the outer edge of the crest is at least equal to the height of the slope;
- (f) if the works are carried out beside the crest of a slope with a gradient of more than 30 degrees –
  - (i) the height of the slope is not more than 3 m; and
  - (ii) the distance between any point of the excavation and the outer edge of the crest is at least equal to 1.5 times the height of the slope; and
- (g) if the works are carried out beside the top of a retaining wall –
  - (i) the height of the wall is not more than 3 m; and
  - (ii) the distance between any point of the excavation and the wall is at least equal to 1.5 times the height of the wall.

### 1.26 — Underground Drain Addition/Alteration
**Addition or alteration of any underground drain**, provided that –
- (a) the works involve an excavation of a depth of more than 1.5 m but not more than 3 m;
- (b) the distance between any point of the excavation and any structure or building is at least equal to the depth of the excavation;
- (c) the works do not involve any excavation within area number 1 or 3 of the scheduled areas;
- (d) the works do not involve the last manhole; and
- (e) if the works are carried out beside the crest of a slope –
  - (i) the gradient of the slope is not more than 15 degrees; (further conditions apply)

### 1.27 — External Canopy
**Erection, alteration or removal of any canopy** projecting from the external wall of a building.

### 1.28 — Metal Supporting Frame for Canopy
**Erection, alteration or removal of any metal supporting frame for a canopy** (associated with item 1.27).

### 1.29 — AC / Cooling Tower Support
**Erection or alteration of any supporting structure for an air-conditioning unit, water cooling tower or any associated air duct** on-grade or on a slab (other than a cantilevered slab).

### 1.30 — Unauthorized Structure Removal (Misc.)
**Removal of any unauthorized structure** (other than an unauthorized floor slab, signboard, or other item specified elsewhere).

### 1.31 — Panel Fixed by Metal Frame
**Erection, repair or removal of any panel fixed by metal supports** on a fence wall or external wall.

### 1.32 — Internal Staircase (Removal)
**Removal of any internal staircase that is not used as a means of escape.**

### 1.33 — Service Lift (Removal)
**Building works associated with the removal of any service lift.**

### 1.34 — Stairlift / Lifting Platform (Removal)
**Building works associated with the removal of any stairlift or lifting platform.**

### 1.35 — Slab Reinstatement
**Reinstatement in accordance with the original design of a slab** after removal of a structure or item.

### 1.36 — Underground Drain (Removal)
**Removal of any underground drain**, provided that –
- (a) the works involve an excavation of a depth of more than 1.5 m but not more than 3 m;
- (b) the distance between any point of the excavation and any structure or building is at least equal to the depth of the excavation;
- (c) the works do not involve the last manhole; and
- (d) further conditions relating to slopes, scheduled areas, and structures apply.

### 1.37 — External Chimney (Removal)
**Removal of any chimney attached to the external wall of a building.**

### 1.38 — Unauthorized Structure (On-grade / Roof)
**Removal of any unauthorized structure located on-grade or on a roof**, provided that the height of the structure is not more than 1.5 m.

### 1.39 — Unauthorized Floor Slab (Removal)
**Removal of any unauthorized floor slab.**

### 1.40 — Metal Gate at Fence Wall/Entrance (Removal)
**Removal of any metal gate at a fence wall or at an entrance to a building.**
