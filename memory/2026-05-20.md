## Class I RSE Requirements (From Discussion)

**When RSE required:**
- Structural calculations, foundation plans, structural details

**Common Class I items needing RSE:**
- Item 1.4: Internal staircases
- Item 1.11/1.12: Large canopies
- RC wall removals/openings
- Item 1.15: Structural repairs
- Item 1.49/1.50: Large supporting structures/water tanks

**RSE-only items (no AP needed):**
- Item 1.25 & 1.26: Structural repairs
- Item 1.34 & 1.35: Columns/beams for AC units
- Item 1.50: RC telecom base station canopy

**Additional requirements:**
- Propping/shoring plans for slab cutting
- Material testing reports (concrete cores, steel tensile tests)
- Form PR4 for submission
